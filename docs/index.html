Why these five first

LC 206 is where tracing stops working and trusting starts. You cannot hold five stack frames in your head, and the page makes that unnecessary: watch five frames pile up changing nothing, then one link flip per return.

LC 509 is where dynamic programming first becomes visible. At n = 30 the recursion makes 2,692,537 calls to compute 31 distinct values. That gap — between distinct subproblems and calls made — is the whole idea, arriving nine weeks before the word is needed.
